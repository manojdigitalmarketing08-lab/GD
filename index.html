Things to Know

How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency

Search online and every agency homepage starts to look the same. Choosing the wrong partner wastes months before anyone admits the strategy isn't working. Here's the checklist we'd hand a friend.

  • Ask for Proof, Not Promises

    Real client numbers — traffic growth, cost per lead, revenue — beat a dashboard screenshot with no context every time.

  • Confirm How They Report

    A good partner ties reporting to pipeline and revenue. A weak one sends a monthly PDF full of impressions.

  • Get Names, Not Job Titles

    Some agencies pitch senior talent, then hand your account to a junior team once the contract is signed.

  • Look for Contract Flexibility

    A confident team shouldn't need a 12-month lock-in to keep you around.

At GD Digital Marketing, our process covers every one of these gaps: senior strategists, live reporting, and month-to-month flexibility after an initial 90-day sprint. Marketers who tie spend to measured performance consistently outperform those who don't, a pattern also documented in Google's own marketing research.
