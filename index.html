Frequently Asked Questions About Digital Marketing Services

Still unsure? Ask a strategist directly.

Talk to a Strategist

What Does a Digital Marketing Agency Do?

A digital marketing agency plans and runs the online channels that bring you customers: search engine optimization, paid ads, social media, email, content, and the website that turns visitors into leads. A good agency ties all of that to one business goal and reports on leads and revenue, not just clicks.

What Services Does a Digital Marketing Agency Provide?

Most full-service agencies provide SEO and local SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads, social media marketing, content marketing, web design and landing pages, email marketing, analytics, and increasingly AI search optimization. GD Digital Marketing offers all of these under one team so every channel works toward the same goal.

How Much Do Digital Marketing Services Cost?

Cost depends on the channels you need, how competitive your market is, and how much work is involved each month. Paid ads also need a separate ad budget. We quote by channel and deliverable so you can see exactly what each part of the fee covers before you commit.

Are Affordable Digital Marketing Services Available for Small Businesses?

Yes. Small businesses rarely need every channel at once. We usually start with the one or two channels most likely to produce leads for your budget, such as local SEO and Google Ads, then add more once those are paying for themselves.

How Do I Find Digital Marketing Services Near Me?

Search for digital marketing agencies in your area, then compare their case studies, reviews, and reporting. Distance matters less than fit: most agency work, including ours, runs smoothly over video calls, shared dashboards, and weekly check-ins.

How Do I Choose a Digital Marketing Agency?

Ask for real case studies, find out who will actually work on your account, check how they report results, and avoid long lock-in contracts. The right agency will explain its strategy before it asks for a commitment.

How Long Does Digital Marketing Take to Show Results?

Paid ads can show early results within a few weeks. SEO and content usually take three to six months to build momentum, then keep compounding. We set channel-by-channel expectations during strategy so there are no surprises.

What Is the Difference Between SEO and Google Ads?

Google Ads buys immediate visibility at the top of search results, and you pay for each click. SEO earns visibility in the organic results over time, and you don't pay per click. Most businesses grow fastest using Ads for quick leads while SEO builds long-term traffic.
