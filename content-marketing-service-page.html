FAQ

Learn More About Our Content Marketing Services

What is content marketing, and how does GD Digital Marketing approach it?

Content marketing means developing high-quality content that connects with your audience wherever they are — your site, blog, social, and email. We take a holistic approach covering strategy, writing, and optimization to drive real ROI, not just publish volume.

Why does content marketing matter for my business?

It's how you get found in search, build trust with prospects before they ever talk to sales, and convert more of that traffic into leads and revenue over time.

How long does it take to see results?

Early ranking signals typically show within 4–8 weeks; meaningful traffic and lead compounding builds over 3–6 months as content earns authority.

What should I budget for content marketing services?

Content should be a meaningful share of your marketing budget since it's the foundation other channels — SEO, email, paid — build on. We'll size a plan to your budget in the proposal, not push a one-size number.

How do you measure content marketing success?

We use GA4 and Search Console to track rankings, traffic, and conversions against your specific goals, then use that data to guide what gets written or refreshed next.
