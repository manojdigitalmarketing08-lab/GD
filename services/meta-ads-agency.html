What does a meta ads agency actually do?

Builds, runs, and optimizes your paid social end to end. A meta ads agency researches your audience, builds Facebook and Instagram campaigns, designs creative, sets budgets, and tracks results, turning ad spend into booked calls and sales instead of just reach.

How much does Meta Ads management cost in India?

₹10,000 to ₹50,000 a month, or 10 to 20% of ad spend. Your actual ad budget goes directly to Meta and is separate from what an agency charges.

How long before Meta Ads starts working?

One to two weeks for meaningful data. Real optimization gains compound over two to three months as we test creative, audiences, and placements against real performance.

What's the difference between Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Interest and behavior versus search intent. Meta Ads reaches people while they're scrolling, while Google Ads captures people already searching. Most businesses run both, since each catches a different stage of the funnel.

Do you work with small businesses too?

Yes. Our facebook advertising agency for small business plans scale with your budget instead of locking you into enterprise pricing or minimum ad spend requirements.

Why should I choose your agency for Meta Ads?

Certified strategists plus reporting you can verify. GD Digital Marketing pairs certified strategists with transparent monthly reporting, so you always know what changed in your account and what it moved, not just a reach report with no explanation.
