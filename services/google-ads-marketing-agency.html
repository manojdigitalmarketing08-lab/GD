What does a google ads marketing agency actually do?

Builds, runs, and optimizes your paid search account end to end. A google ads marketing agency researches keywords, builds and structures campaigns, writes ad copy, sets bids, and tracks results, turning ad spend into booked calls and sales instead of just clicks.

How much does Google Ads management cost in India?

Typically ₹20,000 to ₹3,00,000 a month, or 10-20% of ad spend. Management fees run in that range depending on account size and complexity. Your actual ad budget goes directly to Google and is separate from what an agency charges.

How long before Google Ads starts working?

Usually two to four weeks for meaningful data. Most accounts show meaningful data within that window, with real optimization gains compounding over three to six months as we test bids, copy, and targeting against real performance.

What's the difference between Google Ads and SEO?

Paid placement now versus organic rankings over time. Google Ads puts you at the top of search results immediately through paid placement, while SEO builds organic rankings over months. Most businesses run both, since Ads fills the gap while SEO compounds.

Do you manage Google Shopping and Performance Max too?

Yes: Search, Shopping, Display, and Performance Max. Our google ppc agency work covers all four, with budget shifted toward whichever campaign type is actually driving revenue.

Why should I choose your agency for Google Ads?

Certified strategists plus reporting you can actually verify. GD Digital Marketing pairs certified strategists with transparent monthly reporting, so you always know what changed in your account and what it moved, not just a spend report with no explanation.
