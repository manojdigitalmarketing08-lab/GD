Common Questions

Email Marketing Agency FAQs

What's included when I hire your email management service?

At GD Digital Marketing, we handle strategy, segmentation, copywriting, design, automation, and reporting — the full email marketing agency workflow — so you don't need to hire or manage each role separately in-house.

What should I look for in an email marketing company before signing a contract?

Look past the platform certifications. The best email marketing company will show you past campaign data, explain their segmentation approach, and tell you exactly how they measure success before you sign anything.

What does email marketing agency pricing typically include?

Pricing depends on list size, the number of active flows, and how much custom design work is involved. Most engagements start with a flat monthly retainer, so you know the exact cost before any campaign goes live.

Do you offer cold email outreach for B2B teams, or only newsletters?

Yes — as a cold email marketing agency for B2B pipelines, we build and warm up outbound sequences alongside your regular newsletter and lifecycle campaigns, so both channels work from one shared list strategy.

Can I hire you if there's no email marketing agency near me locally?

Yes. Strategy calls, campaign builds, and reporting all happen remotely over video and email, so location never affects turnaround. Most of our current clients aren't based in the same city as our team.

Can you handle bulk email marketing services for a large, older list?

Yes — large or inactive lists just need extra care first. We clean, re-permission, and warm the list before any high-volume send, so your sender reputation stays protected instead of getting flagged as spam.
