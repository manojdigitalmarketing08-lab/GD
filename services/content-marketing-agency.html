What does a content marketing agency actually do?

Research, strategy, writing, and distribution under one roof. A content marketing agency researches your audience, builds a content strategy, writes and designs the pieces, then distributes and measures them, turning search visibility into leads instead of just traffic.

How much does content marketing cost in India?

Typically ₹30,000 to ₹2,50,000 a month, depending on scope. We build every proposal around your budget first, so you know exactly what's included before signing.

How long does content marketing take to show results?

Three to six months for the first meaningful traffic shift. Most content marketing firms need three to six months before traffic moves meaningfully, with real ROI compounding over nine to eighteen months as older pieces keep ranking and converting.

What's the difference between content marketing and SEO?

SEO handles rankings; content marketing builds what ranks. SEO focuses on technical fixes and rankings, while content marketing builds the actual pieces (guides, blogs, videos) that earn those rankings and hold a reader's attention long enough to convert. Our SEO team works hand in hand with content.

Do you offer white label content marketing for agencies?

Yes, under your own brand. Our white label content marketing bench lets agencies and in-house teams scale output under their own brand, with the same strategists and editors we use on every account.

Why should I choose your agency for content marketing?

In-house strategists plus reporting you can verify. GD Digital Marketing pairs in-house strategists with transparent monthly reporting, so you always know what shipped and what it moved, not just a word count with no explanation.
