What does a google ads marketing agency actually do?

A google ads marketing agency researches keywords, builds and structures campaigns, writes ad copy, sets bids, and tracks results — turning ad spend into booked calls and sales instead of just clicks.

How much does Google Ads management cost in India?

Management fees typically run ₹20,000 to ₹3,00,000 a month, or 10 to 20% of ad spend. Your actual ad budget goes directly to Google and is separate from what an agency charges.

How long before Google Ads starts working?

Most accounts show meaningful data within two to four weeks, with real optimization gains compounding over three to six months as we test bids, copy, and targeting against real performance.

What's the difference between Google Ads and SEO?

Google Ads puts you at the top of search results immediately through paid placement, while SEO builds organic rankings over months. Most businesses run both, since Ads fills the gap while SEO compounds.

Do you manage Google Shopping and Performance Max too?

Yes — our google ppc agency work covers Search, Shopping, Display, and Performance Max, with budget shifted toward whichever campaign type is actually driving revenue.
