FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Content Marketing

What does a content marketing agency actually do?

A content marketing agency researches your audience, builds a content strategy, writes and designs the pieces, then distributes and measures them — turning search visibility into leads instead of just traffic.

How much does content marketing cost in India?

Content marketing services in India typically run ₹30,000 to ₹2,50,000 a month depending on scope. We build every proposal around your budget first, so you know exactly what's included before signing.

How long does content marketing take to show results?

Most content marketing firms need three to six months before traffic moves meaningfully, with real ROI compounding over nine to eighteen months as older pieces keep ranking and converting.

What's the difference between content marketing and SEO?

SEO focuses on technical fixes and rankings, while content marketing builds the actual pieces — guides, blogs, videos — that earn those rankings and hold a reader's attention long enough to convert.

Do you offer white label content marketing for agencies?

Yes — our white label content marketing bench lets agencies and in-house teams scale output under their own brand, with the same strategists and editors we use on every account.

Why should I choose your agency for content marketing?

GD Digital Marketing pairs in-house strategists with transparent monthly reporting, so you always know what shipped and what it moved — not just a word count with no explanation.
