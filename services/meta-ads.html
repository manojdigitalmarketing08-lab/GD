Frequently Asked Questions About Meta Ads

What does a meta ads agency actually do?

A meta ads agency researches your audience, builds Facebook and Instagram campaigns, designs creative, sets budgets, and tracks results — turning ad spend into booked calls and sales instead of just reach.

How much does Meta Ads management cost in India?

Management fees typically run ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 a month, or 10 to 20% of ad spend. Your actual ad budget goes directly to Meta and is separate from what an agency charges.

How long before Meta Ads starts working?

Most accounts show meaningful data within one to two weeks, with real optimization gains compounding over two to three months as we test creative, audiences, and placements against real performance.

What's the difference between Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Meta Ads reaches people based on interests and behavior while they're scrolling, while Google Ads captures people already searching with intent. Most businesses run both, since each catches a different stage of the funnel.
