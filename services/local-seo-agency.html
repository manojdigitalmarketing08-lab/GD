Frequently Asked Questions

What does a local seo agency actually do?

Setup, cleanup, and ongoing management of your listing. We handle Google Business Profile optimization, citation cleanup, local content, and review management so your business ranks in the map pack for searches happening in your actual service area.

How much does local SEO cost?

A flat monthly retainer, scaled to your market. Local seo services pricing typically runs as a flat monthly retainer scaled to your city and competition level. We build every proposal around your budget first, so you know exactly what's included before signing anything.

How long does it take to rank in the map pack?

Usually 60-90 days for first gains. Most businesses start to rank higher on google maps within 60-90 days of a full Google Business Profile cleanup, with steady gains continuing over 6-12 months as citations and reviews compound.

Is Google Business Profile optimization included in local SEO?

Yes, it's a core piece of it. Search engine optimization google my business work is core to any real local plan: categories, services, photos, posts, and Q&A all get optimized alongside your website and citations.

What's the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Scope: one targets a city, the other targets the nation. Regular seo targets broad national rankings, while local search engine optimization focuses on map-pack visibility, citations, and reviews for one specific service area, the way most nearby customers actually search.

Can you handle SEO for multiple business locations?

Yes, through a dedicated multi-location process. Our location based seo process keeps every storefront's listing, citations, and pages consistent, so each location ranks on its own merits instead of competing against your other branches.

Why should I choose your agency for local SEO?

Certified strategists plus reporting you can actually verify. GD Digital Marketing pairs certified local strategists with transparent monthly reporting, so you always know what changed on your profile and what it moved, not just a ranking number with no explanation.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No lock-in. We earn your business every month, not through a contract that traps you, and you work with one dedicated strategist who knows your locations.
